What Is Crypto Trading?

Crypto trading means buying and selling digital assets based on their market price. Users can potentially buy crypto when they believe the price may increase, sell when they believe it may decrease, monitor market prices, manage their portfolio, and review trading activity.

Cryptocurrency prices can move quickly and trading involves risk.

Buy vs Sell

BUY

You purchase an asset. You expect the market price may rise. Your position increases when the asset is purchased.

SELL

You sell an asset you hold. You may sell to reduce a position or exit a trade. The final result depends on the execution price and applicable fees.

Cryptocurrency markets are volatile. Trading involves risk and prices can rise or fall.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is cryptocurrency trading?
Crypto trading is the act of buying and selling digital assets based on their market price. It involves speculation on price movements.
How do I buy a coin?
Select the coin from the markets, enter the amount you wish to buy, review the order and confirm.
How do I sell a coin?
Go to your portfolio, select the coin you hold, enter the amount to sell, review and confirm.
Where can I see my balance?
Your balance is displayed on the Home and Portfolio pages of the dashboard.
Where can I see my trading history?
Trading history is available on the Profile page under Transactions.
How do I log out?
Click the Logout button in the sidebar or on any page.
Are crypto prices always changing?
Yes, cryptocurrency prices fluctuate continuously based on market supply and demand.
Is cryptocurrency trading risk-free?
No. Cryptocurrency trading involves financial risk and losses are possible.
